## I. Introduction

Click2Serve is a digital kiosk and online platform developed to make accessing municipal services in Alaminos City easier, faster, and more convenient for everyone. The system was designed to reduce long queues, minimize confusion about service requirements, and improve the overall experience of residents, visitors, and local government staff when transacting with the city government.

Traditionally, many municipal services required multiple in-person visits, manual form filling, and long waiting times. Click2Serve addresses these challenges by providing clear, step-by-step guidance, service request tracking, and queue management through both a self-service kiosk and a web-based platform. By combining technology with user-centered design, the system helps users understand what services are available, what documents are needed, and how to complete their requests efficiently.

Click2Serve currently focuses on Civil Registry services while also providing general municipal information and guidance for other city offices. The system supports the city's goal of digital transformation and smart governance by improving transparency, accessibility, and service delivery for the community.

### Purpose of This Manual

This User's Manual is designed to guide users in effectively using the Click2Serve system. It provides clear and easy-to-follow instructions for navigating the kiosk and online platform, submitting service requests, tracking applications, and understanding the system's features.

The manual is intended for the following users:
- **Residents and visitors** who wish to request municipal services or access city information
- **LGU staff and service officers** who manage and process service requests
- **System administrators** responsible for maintaining and updating the platform

The contents of this manual include:
- An overview of the Click2Serve system and its purpose
- Step-by-step instructions for using the kiosk and web platform
- Guidance on service request submission, queueing, and tracking
- Descriptions of user roles and system features
- Basic troubleshooting and usage reminders

This manual aims to ensure that all users, regardless of technical skill, can confidently use Click2Serve to access municipal services efficiently and accurately.

## III. Client User Guide - Kiosk

The kiosk interface provides an intuitive, touch-screen experience for accessing municipal services directly at City Hall. This section provides step-by-step instructions for using all kiosk features.

### A. Getting a Service

**Overview:** This process allows clients to submit service requests, receive tracking numbers, and monitor their request progress.

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Access the Kiosk Dashboard**
   - Go to the Click2Serve kiosk at City Hall
   - Tap anywhere on the screen to wake it up if it's in sleep mode
   - The main dashboard will display with three main tiles: "Services", "3D Map & Navigation", and "Municipal Information"

2. **Select Services**
   - Tap the **"Services"** tile/button on the main dashboard
   - You will see a list of available services organized by category

3. **Choose Your Service**
   - Browse through the available services
   - Tap on the service you need (e.g., "Delayed Birth Registration (Regular)" or "Delayed Birth Registration under Birth Registration Assistance Project (BRAP)")

4. **Read the Step-by-Step Process**
   - The system will display detailed information about your selected service
   - Read through the step-by-step process carefully
   - Review the requirements and important notes
   - Tap **"Next"** when you're ready to proceed

5. **Fill Out the Request Form**
   - Enter your **Full Name** (as it appears on your ID)
   - Enter your **Mobile Number** (e.g., 09123456789)
   - Double-check all information for accuracy
   - Tap **"Submit"** to submit your request

6. **Receive Your Reference Number**
   - After submission, you will receive a **Reference Number** (e.g., C2S-20260109-5369)
   - **Important:** Write down or take a photo of this number - you'll need it to track your request
   - You may also receive a **Queue Number** if queue management is enabled

7. **View Service Tracker**
   - After submission, you'll be taken to the **Service Tracker** screen
   - Here you can see:
     - Your reference number
     - Your queue number (if applicable)
     - Current status of your request
     - Next step in the process
   - This screen shows the real-time progress of your request

8. **Return to Home**
   - Tap **"Back to Home"** to return to the main dashboard
   - You can now use the tracking feature to check your request status anytime

9. **Track Your Request**
   - On the main dashboard, locate the **"Enter Reference Code"** field
   - Enter your reference number (e.g., C2S-20260109-5369)
   - Tap the **"TRACK"** button
   - View your request status and progress

**Important Notes:**
- Keep your reference number safe - you'll need it to track your request
- Your request will be processed by the appropriate office
- You may receive SMS notifications about status updates (if enabled)
- Visit the designated office when your status indicates it's ready

## IV. Client User Guide - Web Platform

The web platform provides similar functionality to the kiosk but with the added feature of appointment booking. While the kiosk allows immediate service request submission, the web platform uses an appointment booking system that lets you schedule a specific date and time for your service. This can be accessed from any device with internet connectivity, allowing users to book appointments and track their status from home or anywhere.

### A. Accessing the Web Platform

1. **Open Your Web Browser**
   - Use Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
   - Navigate to the Click2Serve website URL

2. **Navigate the Homepage**
   - The homepage displays similar options to the kiosk
   - You'll see tiles for Services, 3D Map & Navigation, and Municipal Information

### B. Booking an Appointment Online

**Overview:** The web platform uses an appointment booking system that allows you to schedule a specific date and time for your service request. This is different from the kiosk, which submits requests immediately.

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Access Book Appointment**
   - On the website homepage, click **"Book Appointment"** in the navigation menu
   - Or navigate to the Book Appointment page directly

2. **Step 1: Select Service**
   - You'll see a progress indicator showing: **Service** → Time and Date → Details → Done
   - Under **"Civil Registration Services"**, browse available services
   - Click on the service card you need:
     - **Delayed Birth Registration (Regular)**
     - **Delayed Birth Registration under Birth Registration Assistance Project (BRAP)**
   - The selected service will be highlighted

3. **Step 2: Select Time and Date**
   - After selecting a service, click **"Next"** to proceed
   - You'll see a calendar interface
   - **Select Date:**
     - Browse available dates (dates with available slots are highlighted)
     - Click on your preferred date
   - **Select Time:**
     - Available time slots will be displayed for the selected date
     - Click on your preferred time slot
   - Click **"Next"** to continue

4. **Step 3: Enter Details**
   - Fill out the appointment details form:
     - **Full Name:** Enter your complete name (as it appears on your ID)
     - **Mobile Number:** Enter your contact number (e.g., 09123456789)
     - **Email:** Enter your email address (if required)
     - **Additional Information:** Add any relevant notes or special requirements
   - Review all information for accuracy
   - Click **"Next"** or **"Book Appointment"** to proceed

5. **Step 4: Confirmation (Done)**
   - You'll receive a confirmation message
   - **Save your appointment details:**
     - Appointment reference number (if provided)
     - Date and time of appointment
     - Service type
     - Any confirmation code or email
   - You may receive:
     - Email confirmation
     - SMS notification (if enabled)
     - Appointment details on screen

6. **After Booking**
   - Mark your calendar with the appointment date and time
   - Prepare required documents before your appointment
   - Arrive on time for your scheduled appointment
   - Bring your appointment confirmation (if provided)

**Important Notes:**
- Appointments must be booked in advance (availability depends on office schedule)
- You can only book one appointment per service at a time
- Cancellations or rescheduling may be available through the system or by contacting the office
- If you miss your appointment, you may need to book a new one
- Some services may require immediate submission at the kiosk instead of appointment booking

### C. Tracking Your Request Online

1. Enter your reference number in the tracking field on the homepage
2. Click **"TRACK"**
3. View detailed status information
4. Check next steps and requirements

### D. Additional Web Features

- **Service Tracker Page:** Dedicated page for detailed request tracking
- **Appointment Management:** View, reschedule, or cancel your appointments
- **Document Upload:** Upload required documents online (if enabled)
- **Email Notifications:** Receive appointment confirmations and status updates via email
- **Appointment History:** View your past and upcoming appointments

### E. Managing Your Appointments

**Viewing Appointments:**
1. Access your appointment history (if available in your account)
2. See upcoming appointments with date, time, and service type
3. View past appointments and their status

**Rescheduling Appointments:**
1. Find your appointment in the list
2. Click **"Reschedule"** or **"Change Date/Time"**
3. Select a new date and time from available slots
4. Confirm the changes

**Canceling Appointments:**
1. Find your appointment in the list
2. Click **"Cancel"** button
3. Confirm cancellation
4. You may receive a cancellation confirmation

**Note:** Appointment management features may vary. Some appointments may need to be managed by contacting the office directly.

## V. Officer Admin Guide

Officers use the Click2Serve system to manage service requests, update request statuses, handle queues, and process approvals. This section provides comprehensive guidance for officers.

### A. Logging In

1. **Access the Officer Portal**
   - Navigate to `/officer/login` or the officer login URL
   - Enter your **Email** and **Password**
   - Click **"Login"**

2. **Dashboard Overview**
   - After login, you'll see the Officer Dashboard
   - The dashboard displays:
     - Welcome message with your name
     - Statistics overview (total requests, pending, processing, completed)
     - Request management section
     - Today's Queue (if queue management is enabled)
     - Approval requests (for Civil Registry Office)

### B. Dashboard Features

**1. Statistics Overview**
- **Today Requests:** Number of requests received today
- **This Week Requests:** Weekly request count with comparison
- **Status Distribution:** Visual breakdown of requests by status (Pending, Processing, Completed)
- **Weekly Activity Chart:** Bar chart showing daily request activity

**2. Quick Actions**
- **Queue Settings:** Configure queue display and column settings
- **Notifications:** View and manage system notifications
- **Print All Requests:** Generate printable reports
- **Logout:** Secure logout from the system

### C. Request Management

**Overview:** Manage all service requests for your department, update statuses, and track progress.

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Access Request Management**
   - On the dashboard, locate the **"Request Management"** section
   - You'll see tabs for different service types:
     - Delayed Birth Registration (Regular)
     - Delayed Birth Registration under BRAP
     - Other Civil Registry Services
     - Appointments

2. **View Requests**
   - Click on the appropriate tab to view requests
   - The table displays:
     - Client Name
     - Phone Number
     - Reference Number
     - Service Type
     - Status
     - Date Created
     - Actions

3. **View Request Details**
   - Click the **"View"** button (eye icon) next to a request
   - Review complete request information:
     - Client details
     - Service information
     - Status history
     - Documents (if uploaded)
     - Notes and remarks

4. **Update Request Status**
   - Click the **"Update Status"** button (pencil icon) next to a request
   - In the modal that appears:
     - **Tracking Number:** Pre-filled (cannot be changed)
     - **New Status:** Select from dropdown (e.g., Accepted, Registered, Completed)
     - **Status Date:** Select or enter the date
     - **Remarks (Optional):** Add any notes about the status change
   - Click **"Update Status"** to save

5. **Status Workflow**
   - Follow the proper status sequence:
     - Inquiry → Submitted → Accepted → Registered/Completed
     - For BRAP: Additional steps include Posting → Ready for Approval → Registered
   - The system prevents skipping steps or moving backwards

6. **Filter and Search**
   - Use the search function to find specific requests
   - Filter by status, date range, or service type
   - Sort columns by clicking column headers

**Important Notes:**
- Status changes are logged and cannot be reversed
- Some statuses require specific conditions (e.g., posting period completion)
- Always add remarks for important status changes

### D. Queue Management

**Overview:** Manage queue numbers, configure queue display, and monitor today's queue.

**1. Queue Number Settings**

**Accessing Settings:**
- Click **"Queue Settings"** in the dashboard header
- Or navigate to `/officer/queue-number-settings`

**Configuring Queue Settings:**

a. **Enable/Disable Queue Numbers**
   - Toggle the switch to enable or disable automatic queue number generation
   - When enabled, new requests automatically receive queue numbers

b. **Office Display Name**
   - Set a custom name to display on the queue display screen
   - Leave empty to use the default department name

c. **Queue Number Prefix**
   - Set a prefix for queue numbers (e.g., "CRO" for Civil Registry Office)
   - This appears before the queue number (e.g., "CRO #1")

d. **Queue Column Configuration**
   - Configure columns for the queue display
   - **Add Column:**
     - Click to add a new column
     - Enter a **Column Name** (e.g., "On Queue", "To Submit", "Processed")
     - Select **Statuses** that belong to this column (hold Ctrl/Cmd to select multiple)
     - Click **"Remove Column"** to delete a column
   - **Column Settings:**
     - Each column can have multiple statuses assigned
     - Requests with matching statuses appear in that column
     - Columns are displayed in the order they appear in settings

e. **Window Configuration** (if applicable)
   - Add or remove windows
   - Set window names and numbers
   - Enable/disable windows

f. **Save Settings**
   - Click **"Save Settings"** to apply changes
   - Changes take effect immediately

**2. Today's Queue**

**Viewing Today's Queue:**
- On the dashboard, scroll to the **"Today's Queue"** section
- View all requests with queue numbers for today
- Columns display requests categorized by status

**Queue Display Features:**
- **Column Headers:** Show column names and item counts
- **Queue Items:** Display queue number, client name, service type, and time submitted
- **Actions:**
  - Click on a queue item to view details
  - Remove from queue (if needed)
  - Call queue (if window assignment is enabled)

**3. Queue Display Screen**

**Accessing Display Screen:**
- Click **"View Display Screen"** button in Today's Queue section
- Opens a full-screen display for public viewing
- Shows current queue status in real-time

**Display Screen Features:**
- Large, readable queue numbers
- Organized by columns
- Auto-refreshes every 10 seconds
- Shows office name and current date/time
- Color-coded columns for easy identification

### E. Approval Requests (Civil Registry Office Only)

**Overview:** Manage requests that have completed the posting period and are ready for approval.

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Access Approval Requests**
   - Click **"Tap to view [X] Days Posting Requests"** on the dashboard
   - Or navigate to `/officer/approval-requests`

2. **View Posting Requests**
   - See all requests currently in the posting period
   - View statistics:
     - Total in Posting
     - Delayed Regular count and percentage
     - Delayed BRAP count and percentage

3. **Configure Posting Period**
   - Set the number of days for posting period (default: 10 days)
   - Enter the number of days in the input field
   - Click **"Save"**
   - **Important:** Changes only apply to new requests; existing requests keep their original posting period

4. **Review Request Details**
   - Click on a request to view details
   - Check:
     - Posting start date
     - Days remaining
     - Service type
     - Client information

5. **Approve Request**
   - When posting period is complete (Days Remaining = 0):
     - Click **"Approve"** button
     - The status will change to "Registered"
   - If posting period is not complete, approval will be blocked with a message showing days remaining

**Important Notes:**
- Posting period is calculated from the date the request was accepted
- The system validates that the full posting period has elapsed before allowing approval
- Each request maintains its original posting period even if settings change

## VII. Troubleshooting

This section addresses common issues and provides solutions.

### A. Common Client Issues

**Issue: Cannot submit request**
- **Solution:** Check that all required fields are filled
- Ensure internet connection is stable (for web platform)
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact support if problem persists

**Issue: Reference number not received**
- **Solution:** Check your mobile number for SMS
- Check spam folder for email
- Use the tracking feature with your name and phone number
- Contact the office directly

**Issue: Cannot track request**
- **Solution:** Verify reference number is correct
- Check that reference number format matches (e.g., C2S-YYYYMMDD-XXXX)
- Try entering just the tracking number portion
- Contact support with your reference number

**Issue: Kiosk screen not responding**
- **Solution:** Wait a few seconds and try again
- Tap the screen gently
- If unresponsive, contact City Hall staff

### B. Common Officer Issues

**Issue: Cannot log in**
- **Solution:** Verify email and password are correct
- Check that account is active (contact Super Admin)
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Use password reset if available

**Issue: Cannot update request status**
- **Solution:** Check that you're following the correct status workflow
- Verify posting period is complete (for approvals)
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check for error messages

**Issue: Queue not displaying correctly**
- **Solution:** Check queue settings configuration
- Verify column configurations are saved
- Refresh the page
- Check that queue numbers are enabled

**Issue: Reports not generating**
- **Solution:** Check date range selection
- Verify filters are applied correctly
- Try a smaller date range
- Clear browser cache

### C. Common Super Admin Issues

**Issue: Cannot create officer account**
- **Solution:** Verify email is unique
- Check that all required fields are filled
- Ensure password meets requirements
- Check for duplicate email addresses

**Issue: Activity logs not loading**
- **Solution:** Try filtering by date range
- Check server logs for errors
- Refresh the page
- Contact technical support

**For Officers:**

### A. Glossary of Terms

- **BRAP:** Birth Registration Assistance Project
- **Reference Number:** Unique identifier for service requests (format: C2S-YYYYMMDD-XXXX)
- **Queue Number:** Sequential number assigned when queue management is enabled
- **Status:** Current stage of a service request in the workflow
- **Posting Period:** Mandatory waiting period (typically 10 days) for certain services
- **Kiosk:** Self-service terminal located at City Hall
- **Officer:** Staff member who processes service requests
- **Super Admin:** System administrator with full access
